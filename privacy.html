WashBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

WashBook is a daily book for car wash shops. Shop owners and their staff use it to record washes, customers, bills and the day-end cash count. This page explains what information WashBook keeps, why, and how to have it deleted.

Who uses the app

WashBook is used by car wash shops that hold a WashBook licence, and by the staff they add. Each person signs in with a shop code and a personal PIN given by the shop owner. Some shops also offer an online booking page, where their customers can book a time for a wash without signing in. WashBook is not directed at children.

What information is kept

  • Shop details: shop name, shop code, owner name and mobile number, licence dates.
  • Staff details: staff names, roles and PINs, set by the shop owner.
  • Customer details entered by the shop: customer name, mobile number and vehicle number.
  • Online bookings (only for shops that offer them): the customer's name, mobile number, vehicle number, chosen services, day and time, and whether the booking is confirmed, done or cancelled.
  • Wash records: date and time, services, amounts, how the customer paid, amounts due, and the day-end cash count.

WashBook does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It shows no advertising and does not use advertising or tracking tools.

Why it is kept

Only to run the shop's daily book: to list today's washes and bookings, find returning customers, prepare bills, show reports to the owner, and check the licence. A customer's booking details are shown only to that shop, which may contact the customer on WhatsApp about the booking.

Where it is stored

Each shop's records are kept in a Google Sheet run by WashBook, using Google's servers (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets). Information travels between the app and Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The phone also keeps a copy of the shop's recent records so the app works without internet. The app locks behind the staff PIN, and this copy is removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Sharing

WashBook does not sell or rent any information, and does not share it with anyone except Google, which hosts it. When a shop sends a bill or an offer to a customer on WhatsApp, the shop's own phone opens WhatsApp and the staff member chooses to send it; WashBook does not send WhatsApp messages by itself. When an owner asks to renew, the request is emailed to WashBook.

How long it is kept

Records are kept while the shop holds a WashBook licence, so the owner can see past days and customers. After a shop stops using WashBook, its records are deleted within 90 days, or sooner on request.

Deleting information

  • A shop owner can delete a customer, a staff member or a wash record from the shop's Google Sheet, or ask WashBook to do it.
  • To delete a whole shop account and all its records, the owner can email carwashapp@gmail.com or send a WhatsApp message to +91 76750 27103, giving the shop name and shop code. The account and all its records are deleted within 30 days and we confirm when it is done.
  • A customer of a shop who wants their details removed can ask the shop, or contact WashBook at the same address. A customer can also cancel their own booking from the booking page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
